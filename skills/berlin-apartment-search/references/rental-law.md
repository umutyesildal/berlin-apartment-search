# Rental law

Data-as-of: 2026-11-22. **Orientation, not legal advice.**

For a qualifying new tenancy in an area with a valid Mietpreisbremse designation,
§556d BGB generally limits initial rent to 10% above the local comparative rent.
Do not equate asking prices with that comparative rent. Verify Berlin's current
designation and the tenancy's date before applying the rule.

Berlin's Senate adopted a new designation on 11 November 2025 for the whole
city, effective 1 January 2026 through 31 December 2029. Check for later changes
when applying this guidance to a future tenancy.

Exceptions/adjustments matter: §556e covers a qualifying higher prior rent and
recent modernization; §556f excludes dwellings first used and let after
1 October 2014 and the first letting after comprehensive modernization.
§556g regulates information duties and complaints. Furnishing alone is not a
blanket exemption. Some genuinely temporary or landlord-occupied arrangements
have separate applicability issues under §549; a label in an ad is not proof.

§551 caps residential security at three monthly rents excluding stated operating
costs. A cash deposit can be paid in three monthly installments, the first due
at tenancy start. Money must be held separately as required by that provision.
Do not pay a “reservation fee” to see a flat. For a suspected violation, obtain
individual advice before withholding rent or sending a legal demand.
