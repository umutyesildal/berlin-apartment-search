# Districts and commute

Data-as-of: 2026-11-22. **Orientation, not legal advice.**

Berlin has 12 administrative boroughs (Bezirke); neighborhoods/Ortsteile are not
interchangeable with boroughs. Use an actual address to assess transit, access
needs and noise, not borough-wide stereotypes or assumed safety scores.
Confirm door-to-door travel at the relevant time with BVG/VBB. Listing location
labels and approximate map pins may be inaccurate. P0 does not calculate commute
times or geocode addresses. Do not infer resident characteristics from location.

Sources (official city and transit information, consulted 2026):
- https://www.berlin.de/berlin-im-ueberblick/bezirke/
- https://www.bvg.de/en/connections
- https://www.vbb.de/fahrinformation/
