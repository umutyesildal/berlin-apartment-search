# Mietspiegel: comparative rent and costs

Data-as-of: 2026-11-22. **Orientation, not legal advice.**

The Berlin Senate published the qualified Mietspiegel 2026 on 28 May 2026,
replacing the 2024 edition. Its reference date is 1 September 2025. Determine the
applicable field and position using building age, dwelling size, features and
official residential location. Check coverage/exclusions in the official guide.
A citywide median is **not** a flat's legally relevant comparative rent.

The skill intentionally bundles no numeric Mietspiegel table. Supply an applicable
€/m² value and dated source yourself. Without both, the script leaves the +10%
comparison unknown. Even a comparison with an applicable value is conditional
on tenancy coverage and exceptions. Source year is not the tenancy date.

Cold rent (Nettokaltmiete) excludes operating costs. Warm rent often includes
heating advances, but verify exactly what it includes; electricity and internet
may be separate. Do not subtract an unspecified “price” to invent cold rent.
Ancillary costs depend on included services and energy use. The script's
0.80–6.00 €/m²/month band is only a deliberately broad review heuristic,
not a legal threshold or an official Berlin forecast.
