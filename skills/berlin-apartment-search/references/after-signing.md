# After signing

Data-as-of: 2026-11-22. **Orientation, not legal advice.**

Arrange a documented handover: condition/photos, defects, meter readings, keys
and dates. Keep the signed contract and payment evidence privately. Clarify
utilities and heating responsibilities, internet and any household broadcasting
contribution. Do not cancel an existing tenancy based merely on an unverified ad.

Registration after moving into a dwelling is generally required within two
weeks under §17 BMG, subject to statutory exceptions. Obtain the
Wohnungsgeberbestätigung (housing-provider confirmation) under §19 BMG; it is
not simply a substitute name for a rental contract. Confirm current appointment
procedures with Berlin's service portal. P0 books nothing for you.
