# WBS: Wohnberechtigungsschein

Data-as-of: 2026-11-22. **Orientation, not legal advice.**

A WBS certifies eligibility for specified subsidized housing; it is not a flat
allocation or guarantee. Match the ad's WBS type, permitted household/room size
and any special-need condition. Apply through the responsible housing office.
Ask the authority about residence status and household details rather than
assuming every newcomer qualifies or is excluded.

The Berlin Senate's 2025 guidance lists WBS 140 annual assessable-income limits
of €16,800 for one person and €25,200 for two; extra household members and children
have additions. WBS 160/180 and 220 can apply to particular funding programs.
These are **not gross-salary cutoffs** and not universal housing eligibility
limits: deductions and household composition matter. Use the official calculator
and housing office; P0 does not decide eligibility.
