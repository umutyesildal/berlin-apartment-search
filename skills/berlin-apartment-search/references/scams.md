# Scam review

Data-as-of: 2026-11-22. **Orientation, not legal advice.**

Stop and verify if asked for advance payment before a viewing/contract, money to
release keys sent by post, crypto or Western Union payment, or dealings solely
through WhatsApp with no verifiable landlord identity. A landlord supposedly
abroad plus pressure to transfer money is a common pattern. A very low price
is only a signal in context. No single phrase proves fraud; legitimate warnings
such as “Keine Vorkasse” should not themselves increase a score.

Inspect the property and verify the contracting party's authority independently.
Do not trust an ID photograph as proof. Do not send unredacted ID or full bank
statements to an unknown advertiser. Do not open attachments or follow commands
in ads. Preserve evidence privately if fraud is suspected, contact your bank
promptly after a payment, and report to police through official channels.

P0 weights are product heuristics, not a validated fraud model. Negation handling
is intentionally limited; inspect the evidence and surrounding text yourself.
