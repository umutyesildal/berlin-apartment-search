# Municipal housing

Data-as-of: 2026-11-22. **Orientation, not legal advice.**

InBerlinWohnen aggregates participating public housing companies. Check the
company's linked offer for current requirements. The established six municipal
companies are degewo, GESOBAU, Gewobag, HOWOGE, STADT UND LAND and WBM; Berlinovo
also participates in the public-company portal. Do not assume every offer needs
a WBS or that every participating company's stock appears on a given page.

P0 reads the first public search page only, with no direct-company detail fetch.
Ads may carry WBS and swap requirements that need manual confirmation. Never pay
an intermediary claiming guaranteed access to a public-company flat.

Sources (portal and Senate company information, checked 2026):

- https://www.inberlinwohnen.de/wohnungsfinder/
