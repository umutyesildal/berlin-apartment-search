# Newcomers

Data-as-of: 2026-11-22. **Orientation, not legal advice.**

Separate Kaltmiete (base rent), Nebenkosten (operating costs), Warmmiete (total
as stated, often including heating advances) and Kaution (security deposit).
Anmeldung is address registration, not permission to reside in Germany.
Ask whether the provider will issue the legally required confirmation for your
actual arrangement; do not buy a fictitious registration address.

Sublets need careful checks of permission, duration, actual contracting party
and deposit arrangements. “Temporary” or “furnished” does not by itself settle
the legal rules. Never invent income or visa status to improve an application.
Ask which alternative evidence a landlord accepts if you lack local paperwork.
For residency issues use the Berlin immigration office or qualified advice, not
an ad's promise. Local drafts use only volunteered facts; the tools never send
them or contact advertisers. Optional self-notifications are separate from applications.
